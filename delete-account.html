Delete your Prompt Heart account

Prompt Heart, by Inigo. You can delete your account and its data at any time.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Prompt Heart and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings → Profile.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Your account is deleted immediately. You can choose whether to keep a copy of your prompts on the phone or erase them too.

Without the app

Email inigo@tosstheworld.org from the email address you signed up with, with the subject "Delete my Prompt Heart account". Your account and its data will be deleted within 30 days, and you'll get an email when it's done.

What gets deleted

  • Your account: email address, password and name
  • All your synced prompts, categories and tags
  • All images stored in the cloud, and your profile photo

Nothing is kept on my servers after deletion. Deleted data may stay in my hosting provider's routine backups for a short time before they're overwritten.

Prompts kept on your phone after you delete your account stay there until you remove them or uninstall the app.
