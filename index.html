Why it exists

Good prompts get lost in chats, notes and screenshots. Prompt Heart keeps the ones that work in one place, ready for whichever AI app you open today.

What it won’t do

No ads, no tracking and nothing to buy. Your prompts stay on your phone unless you choose to sync them, and you can delete your account at any time.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes, completely. There are no ads, no subscriptions and no paid extras.

Do I need an account?

No. Everything works offline without one. An account only adds sync, so your prompts and categories follow you to another phone.

Where are my prompts kept?

On your phone. If you sign in, a copy is kept in your private account so your devices stay in sync. The privacy policy lists exactly what is stored and where.

Which AI apps does it work with?

ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok, Perplexity, Copilot, DeepSeek and Le Chat. ChatGPT, Claude, Grok and Perplexity open with your prompt already typed in; for the others it’s on your clipboard, ready to paste.

What are the blanks in a prompt?

Write {{topic}} where a prompt changes each time, or {{tone|friendly}} to give it a default. When you copy, Prompt Heart asks for each blank and fills them in.

How do I get updates?

From F-Droid, updates arrive like any other app. If you installed the APK, Prompt Heart tells you when a new version is out.

Is it on Google Play or iPhone?

It’s an Android app. For now you install it from F-Droid or the APK here; Google Play is next. There is no iPhone version.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, open Settings, tap Cloud sync, then Delete account. You can also ask for it to be deleted without the app.
