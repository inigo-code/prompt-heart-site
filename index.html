Prompt Heart

Your AI prompts, one tap away.

Prompt Heart is a notes-style app for the prompts that work. Save them, fill in {{placeholders}}, copy in one tap, and open ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and more. It works offline, and an optional account syncs your prompts across devices.
